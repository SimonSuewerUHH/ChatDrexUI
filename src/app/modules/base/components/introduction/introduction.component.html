ChatDRex: A Conversational Multi-Agent Platform

Introduction

ChatDRex leverages a novel multi-agent architecture built on LangChain and LangGraph to facilitate complex, network-based drug repurposing analyses through a conversational interface. This approach mitigates biases and hallucinations in Large Language Models (LLMs) by integrating directly with validated biomedical databases and specialized bioinformatics tools, ensuring precise and reliable analyses.

Key Features

  • Multi-Agent Orchestration
    A directed graph structure coordinates specialized agents, each fulfilling distinct roles in query processing.
  • Validated Biomedical Data
    Direct integration with established databases ensures high-quality, reliable results.
  • Functional Enrichment Analysis
    The DIGEST Node enables researchers to conduct comprehensive gene set analyses to uncover relevant biological pathways.
  • Network-Based Drug Repurposing
    Access NeDRexDB to analyze disease-related networks and identify promising drug targets.
  • Comprehensive Literature Retrieval
    The Research Node queries Semantic Scholar for relevant publications to support findings and hypotheses.

ChatDRex streamlines drug repurposing research by providing an interactive, AI-driven framework that simplifies complex analyses. By leveraging an agent-based architecture and validated biomedical resources, it empowers researchers with an intuitive, reliable, and scalable solution for exploring therapeutic opportunities.
