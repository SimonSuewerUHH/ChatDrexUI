Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is ChatDRex?

ChatDRex is a conversational multi-agent platform designed to facilitate complex, network-based drug repurposing analyses. It integrates validated biomedical databases and specialized bioinformatics tools, ensuring high accuracy and reliability of results.

How does ChatDRex handle multiple agents?

ChatDRex uses a multi-agent architecture orchestrated by the Start Node. Each query is analyzed and then directed to specialized agents—such as the LLM Node, DIGEST Node, Network Node, or Research Node—depending on the user’s request. This design enhances efficiency and accuracy through task specialization.

How does ChatDRex address potential hallucinations from LLMs?

ChatDRex incorporates a two-stage validation system: the Hallucination Chain and the Grading Chain. The Hallucination Chain cross-verifies generated content with validated references, while the Grading Chain evaluates the clarity, correctness, and relevance of the response, ensuring high-quality and factually accurate outputs.

Can I use ChatDRex without coding experience?

Yes. ChatDRex is designed as a conversational interface, enabling researchers and scientists to interact naturally using plain language. Its multi-agent system manages complex tasks under the hood, so you can perform network-based analyses without needing specialized programming skills.

How do I integrate ChatDRex into my research workflow?

ChatDRex can be accessed through its web-based interface, allowing you to submit queries related to gene interactions, enrichment analyses, or literature searches. It also features built-in tools like DIGEST and NeDRexDB for specialized functionalities. Simply pose your questions, and ChatDRex routes them to the relevant agents.

How do I cite ChatDRex in my publication?

Please cite ChatDRex using the relevant references and links provided in the documentation or on the ChatDRex platform. Including details about the version used, date accessed, and any related tool references helps maintain accurate and transparent reporting of your research methods.
